— A note from the author —

This site is a working hypothesis. The argument is that the gap between what one person can ship and what a team can ship has narrowed in a way that deserves a name, a discipline, and a defense.

The three artifacts here are an attempt to claim the names — NeoArchitect, NeoArchitecture — and to describe the practice clearly enough that the people already doing the work can recognize themselves in it, and the organizations already cutting those people can see what they are losing.

Each artifact was itself built using the discipline it describes.
